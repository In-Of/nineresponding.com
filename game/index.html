Game demo coming soon

The Nine Responding game is the same world as the stories — only this time you're the one behind the wheel. On the edge of settled space, a distress call comes in from an empty planet. The big ships can't land there — it's too dangerous. The only way in is the rover: an old six-wheeled machine on long legs, barely holding together.

In the stories, Relic answers calls like this. In the game the rescuer has no name and no voice: his character is your choices.

There's no map marker. The signal from the stories is one you have to hear for yourself: it only gets clearer when your nose points the right way. You turn, you listen, you drive. The terrain is the hard part. A bad climb eats energy, and push the machine too hard on a bad slope and something gives out — not because the game decided it was time, but because you did that.

Between calls, the planet is yours. A calm free drive with no combat and no grind: old ruins, anomalies, scrap worth hauling home to keep the machine running. Park, and the reactor tops up the cells.

The demo is nearly ready, with both a rescue mission and free driving. When it's out, the link will appear here and in the news.
