About

Echo

For more than thirty years I worked in IT. Recently I lost my job and haven't found a new one yet. But I suddenly had free time, and it turned out that a lot of creative ideas had built up inside me. I started giving them shape and couldn't stop: now it takes 12–14 hours a day. It's a full-time job, and I want to give myself to it completely.

It all started a few years ago with a song. Then came a music video. And then I realized a whole world could grow out of it: stories, characters, a game. I'm now finishing the game's demo.

To be honest, I get help from AI. It draws, because I'm not an artist, and with the writing it helps me connect episodes, catch contradictions and add believable details. But the plots, characters and ideas are entirely mine. For me it's a tool, an assistant, as if I'd hired someone to draw an illustration, not a co-author.

Right now this is my only source of income. If you like what I do, I'm asking for your support. It will let me keep creating and bring you new stories.
